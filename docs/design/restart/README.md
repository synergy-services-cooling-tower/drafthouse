## Summary

Decision: The public site is a set of Blocks tiles on its light default (greys, ink as the only accent, square corners, dot grid). The 3D tower is the hero: a cutaway drawn live from the engine's geometry, with falling water shaded from hot to cold and air moving through it. Before it loads, an isometric line-drawing poster of the same tower stands in. The wizard keeps the tower beside the form at 1440 and puts it in a strip above the form at 390. The full tower view has controls on the left and the picked part's card, with each dimension's formula, on the right. At 390 these become a bottom sheet.
Screens: the landing page, wizard step 1 (duty), and the full 3D tower view. Each is drawn at 1440 and 390, plus the landing page's first paint (the poster) at 390.
Open questions: (1) The results/ranking, calc-sheet, wizard steps 2 and 3, and embedded-host mockups were left out to keep this round to 3 screens: should they come next, as a second design round, before impl starts? (2) The nozzles are cut away with the walls, though the ADR keeps them; left whole, they floated outside the casing. Is that OK?

## What impl takes from here

- Look: Blocks' light default pair and tile style only (`site.css` restates its tokens, it adds no colour). The state colours are used small: ochre marks the synthetic-catalog notice and "updating", brick red marks a refusal, moss green marks "rated". Primary buttons are ink.
- Tower materials, built from numbers alone: concrete basin, light casing, tan fill layers, grey-green drift blades, dark fan, water in the ink teal (`--c`). Water particles shade from ochre (hot) to teal (cold); air particles are a muted blue. The cutaway is one plane parallel to the front face; the fan and particles stay whole.
- Numbers are the engine's at the default duty (720 m³/h, 42 → 32 °C, wet bulb 27 °C, sea level), read from the recorded `select` step in `validation/regression-baseline.json`. Rank 1 under "least over-capacity" is IDCF-064 · FILM-VF38 1.2 m · DE-4P-ULTRA · AX-500 at 88 %. The slider bounds are placeholders for the engine's own input ranges.
- Files: `landing.html`, `wizard.html` and `tower.html` are the pages; `site.css` is the page style. `fixture.js` holds the default duty, the top three designs and a `TowerGeometry` in shapes.md's shape. `tower.js` is a preview-only Three.js port of tower3d.md's part mapping, and `poster.js` draws the SVG poster. None of them is product code.
- `./render.sh [page…]` renders every page at 1440 and 390 with Playwright's cached headless Chromium, with WebGL by SwiftShader. It loads Three.js from `web/vendor/`, so rerun it before W-phase deletes that folder, or point the import map at the new package.
