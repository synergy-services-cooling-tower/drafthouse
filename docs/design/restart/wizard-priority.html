What matters most?

Every feasible tower holds the duty. Pick how to rank them; you can change it on the results.

Least over-capacity

The tower that holds the duty with the smallest reserve: no more cell, fill or fan than needed.

Lowest electrical input

The fan that draws the least power at the operating point. Favours deeper fill and slower fans.

Lowest make-up water

The least water lost to evaporation, drift and blowdown. Favours low-drift eliminators.

Lowest air-side pressure

The smallest pressure the fan works against. Favours open fill and large air inlets.
