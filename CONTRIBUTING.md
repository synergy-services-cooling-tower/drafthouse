# Contributing

This project is published as a **reference and teaching implementation**. It is not currently
accepting contributions, feature requests, or issue reports, and Issues and Discussions are
disabled for that reason.

That is a deliberate posture rather than a lack of interest. A
project that looks maintained, gathers users, and then goes quiet is worse for everyone than one
that states its boundaries plainly up front.

You are free to fork and modify under the terms of the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) — study, research, teaching, personal and other noncommercial use are all permitted. Commercial use needs a licence from Synergy Services Co., Ltd.

## Before you rely on this

Read `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` and `VALIDATION_RESULTS.md` first. In particular: the bundled
component catalog is entirely synthetic, and the air-side model has never been compared against a
real tower. The validation status of each area is recorded in `docs/VALIDATION.md` §8.
