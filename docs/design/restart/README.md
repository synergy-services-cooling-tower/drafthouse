## Summary

Decision: The site wears the synergyservices.co.th look on Blocks tokens: dark carbon by default with a light toggle, one green accent, Montserrat, 8px corners, hairlines and no shadows, a dot-field header and a brand-green footer. The tower is an isometric line drawing in thin green edges: no fills and no shadow. Only the water (hot to cold) and the air are in colour, and the poster is the same drawing. The layout and content are unchanged from the last round.
Screens: landing (dark, light and poster), wizard steps 1 to 3, results and ranking, design and calc sheet, full tower view, and the wizard inside a host app. Each is drawn at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) The water and air colours (orange to blue, pale cyan) are the only colours outside the website's scale. OK? (2) Embedded, the notice reads "catalog from the host app"; should the host's real catalog also say "not CTI certified"? (3) The ADR's screens.md still says "light only, no theme switch"; impl should follow this round.

## What impl takes from here

- Tokens: `site.css` restates the website's scale (3 carbons, 3 greens, haze, paper, ink; 6 type steps, 6 spacing steps, one 8px radius) and maps them to semantic names (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--accent`, `--rule`…). Dark is on `:root`, light on `.light`. Pages use the semantic names only. Added beyond the website: `--island` (the tower ground), `--flow-hot`, `--flow-mid`, `--flow-cold`, `--flow-air`, and `--warn` and `--bad` for the notice and refusals.
- Tower: `tower.js` is a preview port of tower3d.md as line art. Each part is drawn with `EdgesGeometry` lines in `--accent` plus a depth-only occluder mesh, so hidden lines drop out. Fill sheets, louvres and casing seams are fainter lines, and the cut-away casing stays as a faint ghost outline. Water is short vertical streaks, coloured hot through mid to cold by height; air is short streaks along the flow. The camera is orthographic isometric, matching the website's drawings, and a picked part draws in `--accent-hi` while the rest dims.
- Numbers: the default duty (200 kg/s, 42 → 32 °C, wet bulb 27, dry bulb 33, sea level) and the top 8 by least over-capacity come from the recorded `select` step in `validation/regression-baseline.json`. This round corrects the last round's fixture: ranks 2 and 3, a capability of 1.093 (not 1.003), nozzle NZ-20 and dry bulb 33 °C. Every calc-sheet substitution was checked against the recorded values. The fan curve's shape is drawn for layout only; impl plots the engine's curve points. Slider bounds stand in for the engine's input ranges.
- Charts: a single hue, values in text colour, no legend for one series, and a dashed neutral line for the system curve, so it never relies on colour alone.
- Files: one HTML page per screen. `chrome.js` holds the header, the footer and the `?light` theme; `wizard.css` and `wizard.js` hold the shared rail and side column; `fixture.js`, `poster.js` and `tower.js` are preview only. `fonts/` holds the website's self-hosted Montserrat and Noto Sans Thai (SIL OFL).
- `./render.sh [page…]` renders every page at 1440 and 390, plus the light and poster landings, with Playwright's cached headless Chromium and WebGL by SwiftShader. It loads Three.js from `web/vendor/`, so rerun it before the W phase deletes that folder, or point the import map at the new package.
